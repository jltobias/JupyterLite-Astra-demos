# JupyterLite Astra Demos

This JupyterBook accompanies the browser-executable notebooks in **JupyterLite Astra Demos**.

The examples are original educational reinterpretations inspired by selected entries in the [Awesome GPT-6 Astra Demos](https://github.com/magiccreator-ai/awesome-gpt-6-astra) community catalog. They do not reproduce creator code, prompts, assets, or benchmark claims.

## Launch JupyterLite

Open the complete browser IDE: [JupyterLite Lab](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-Astra-demos/lite/lab/index.html).

## Chapters

1. **Aquarium motion model** — simple bounded particle motion inspired by Tim Jayas's Reference-Image Aquarium Game.
2. **Robot duel dynamics** — pursuit, dashing, and energy state inspired by Universe Duel.
3. **Procedural train assembly** — parametric component placement inspired by Tom Krcha's Procedural Train Assemblies.
4. **Glass drying model** — a first-order evaporation comparison inspired by Barron Roth's Glass-Drying Physics Simulation.
5. **Model railroad scheduler** — route blocks and discrete-event scheduling inspired by Probably Nick's Interactive Model Railroad.
